Privacy Policy

Havemul dealer app · Haveri Milk Union

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how Haveri District Co-operative Milk Producers' Societies Union Limited ("Haveri Milk Union", "we", "us") collects and uses information through the Havemul mobile app and the havemul.in website. The app is for dealers and agents registered with the union.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details provided when the union registers you as a dealer: name, business/agency name, mobile number, email (if given), address, GST number, delivery route and zone.
  • Order and account information: indents and orders you place, invoices, wallet balance, credit limit, ledger and payment history.
  • Payment information: online payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive the payment status and reference number. We do not receive or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank login details.
  • App settings: preferences such as language and the morning reminder.
  • Sign-in data: login tokens are stored securely on your device so you stay signed in.

The app does not access your location, contacts or microphone, and does not contain advertising or third-party analytics/tracking tools. The camera or photo library is used only if you choose to scan or pick a UPI QR code inside the Razorpay payment screen.

2. How we use information

  • To let you place and manage orders and indents, and to dispatch and deliver products on your route.
  • To generate invoices, maintain your account ledger and process payments.
  • To send order, payment and reminder notifications you have enabled.
  • To meet legal, tax (GST) and audit obligations of the union.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Sharing

We share information only as needed to run the service:

  • Razorpay, to process online payments (see Razorpay's privacy policy).
  • Our hosting and infrastructure providers, which store data on our behalf.
  • Government or regulatory authorities when required by law.

4. Storage and security

Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and stored on secured servers with access limited to authorised union staff. Order, invoice and payment records are kept for as long as required by applicable accounting and tax laws.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You can view your profile, orders, invoices and payments in the app.
  • To correct your details, contact the union office. Some fields require admin approval.
  • Account deletion: dealer accounts are created by the union, so deletion is handled by the union office. Contact us using the details below to request deletion of your account and personal data. We will delete it except for records we must keep by law, such as tax invoices.
  • You can turn off the morning reminder at any time in the app's Profile screen.

6. Children

The app is intended for registered business dealers and is not directed at children under 18.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date it was last updated.
