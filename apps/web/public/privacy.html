Privacy Policy

Haveri District Co-operative Milk Producers' Societies Union Limited ("Haveri Milk Union", "we", "our" or "us") operates the Havemul mobile application (the "App") and the website havemul.in. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use and protect information about dealers and agents who use the App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

The App is available only to dealers registered with Haveri Milk Union. Dealer accounts are created by the union, and we hold the information provided at registration, which may include your name, agency or business name, mobile number, email address, postal address, GST number, and assigned delivery route and zone.

When you use the App, we also record the orders and indents you place, invoices, wallet and credit balances, account ledger entries, payment records, and the preferences you set in the App, such as language and reminder settings.

The App does not collect your location, contacts or browsing activity, and it does not use advertising or third-party analytics services.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • process orders and indents and arrange dispatch and delivery on your route;
  • prepare invoices and maintain your account with the union;
  • process and record payments;
  • send notifications about orders, payments and reminders that you have enabled;
  • respond to your requests and provide support; and
  • comply with legal, tax and audit requirements.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Disclosure of Information

We disclose information only where necessary to provide the service, namely to our payment processor (see Section 4), to service providers who host and maintain our systems on our behalf, and to government or regulatory authorities where required by law.

4. Payments

Online payments in the App are processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited. Your card, UPI or bank details are entered directly with Razorpay and are not received or stored by us. We receive only the payment status, amount and transaction reference. Razorpay's handling of your information is governed by its privacy policy.

5. Device Permissions

The App may request access to your camera or photo library only when you choose to scan or select a UPI QR code during payment. This access is optional, and images are not uploaded to or stored by us. Sign-in credentials are kept in your device's secure storage so that you remain signed in.

6. Data Retention and Security

Information is transmitted using encrypted connections and stored on secured servers, with access limited to authorised personnel of the union. We retain account and transaction records for as long as your account is active and thereafter for the period required under applicable accounting and tax laws.

7. Your Rights and Account Deletion

You may view your profile, orders, invoices and payment history within the App. To correct your details, or to request deletion of your account and personal information, please contact the union office using the details in Section 10. We will act on your request within a reasonable time, except for records that we are required to keep by law, such as tax invoices.

You may switch off reminder notifications at any time from the Profile screen in the App.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for business use by registered dealers and is not directed at persons under the age of 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
